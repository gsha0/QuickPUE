## Findings for the test-case owners

1. **O-07 jacket heater EFLH.** The stated ramp (full output below 10 C, off at 32 C, linear between) gives
   5,777 h from the test EPW, not 3,680 h. No simple variant reproduces 3,680 h (daily mean 5,859 h, daily
   maximum 3,615 h, ramp 0 to 32 C 4,057 h). The cases therefore use the stated EFLH as an input
   (`eflh_hours: 3680`), which reproduces 1,119 MWh and 3,474 MWh (S-05). Suggest recording this as a new
   inconsistency (I-O11) or correcting the ramp definition.
2. **O-10 wet bulb.** The psychrometric wet bulb from the EPW dry bulb, dew point and station pressure at
   18 January 13:00 is 23.2 C, not 24.0 C. With the stated 24.0 C the formula reproduces 27.8 / 32.8 C
   exactly; the engine hour gives 32.2 C at the condenser.
3. **O-11.** 157 h is reproduced with `T_db > 31 C`. Three hours are exactly 31.0 C, so `>=` gives 160 h.
4. **W-01 April RH.** Hourly mean 73.0 % vs 72 % in the table; all other months agree within rounding.
5. **O-03 / I-O9 confirmed.** Interpolated efficiency at 90 % (96.8 %) gives 3.20 % of IT, the source used
   3.00 % (the 100 % point). 75 / 50 / 25 % agree within 0.2 points.
6. **Non-office lighting.** The stated inputs (5 W/m2 over 21,875 m2 with the 5 % / 15 % profile, plus plant
   areas) give about 106 MWh/yr; the reference row is 861.3 MWh, which implies about 98 kW continuous. Suggest
   adding this as an inconsistency or recording the lighting basis used in the source.
7. **DC_S1 S-06.** With the library curve analogue (rating conditions missing) the 20 duty chillers per loop
   deliver about 23,060 kW at the design ambient (35.4 C) + 2.7 K against about 24,590 kW required (IT,
   air-side gains, fan and pump heat): margin about -6 %, so standby units run in about 130 h at 90 % IT and
   the plant reports about 120 unmet hours. The result depends on the missing chiller data.
